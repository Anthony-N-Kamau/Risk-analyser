Methodology & data sources

Every figure carries a label. User assumption: what you type. Model calculation: computed by a deterministic, unit-tested engine (version shown with each result). Illustrative default: a placeholder used when you leave a field blank, always disclosed. AI estimate: the risks, their probabilities and impacts in the risk register, and the written text.

Data sources

No external economic or market data is used in this version. ECB, Eurostat, World Bank and FRED are not yet connected, so nothing here is real-time.

Formulas

  • Cash balance(t) = starting cash + cumulative (revenue - operating costs). Revenue grows by your monthly growth rate after any launch delay.
  • NPV = -initial investment + sum of monthly net cash flows discounted at (1 + annual rate)^(1/12) - 1. No terminal value is assumed.
  • IRR is found numerically on the same flows and shown as annualised. If cash flows never change sign it is reported as not meaningful.
  • ROI = (total net cash flow - initial investment) / initial investment. EBITDA* is revenue minus operating costs, before tax and depreciation.
  • Monte Carlo: 10,000 paths with a fixed seed. Monthly revenue and cost shocks are lognormal and mean-preserving, with an illustrative correlation of 0.3 and cost volatility of 10%. Outcome = total net cash flow minus initial investment.
  • VaR is the loss at the chosen percentile of simulated outcomes. CVaR is the average loss beyond it. Parametric VaR assumes normal outcomes.
  • Stress scenarios change revenue, costs, launch timing and the discount rate, then recompute everything. They do not scale probabilities.
  • Expected loss (register) = sum of probability x impact x capital, using AI-estimated probabilities.

Limits

Taxes, depreciation, debt, working capital and balance sheet ratios are not modelled yet. Results are not backtested. The first view is a sample with illustrative figures. This is educational decision support, not financial advice or regulated analysis.
