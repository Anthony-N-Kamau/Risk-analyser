      Methodology & data sources

      Each risk is given a probability (1 to 99%) and an impact (% of starting capital lost if it happens) by an AI model, based only on your description and any parameters you enter. No live market, price or company data is used, so treat every number as an informed estimate to challenge.

      How the numbers are calculated

      • Exposure = probability × impact, per risk.
      • Overall score = sum of exposures, capped at 100. Labels: under 20 Low, under 45 Moderate, under 70 Elevated, else Critical.
      • Severity: exposure 10 or more High, 4 or more Medium, otherwise Low.
      • Capital at risk: from the probability-weighted total up to the combined impact of all risks with probability of 50% or more.
      • The stress slider scales every probability up or down.

      Limits

      Risks overlap, so totals are approximate. The sample shown at first load is illustrative only.
